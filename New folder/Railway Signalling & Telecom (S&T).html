Railway Infrastructure (S&T)

At SINHA INFRASTRUCTURE AND DEVELOPMENT PRIVATE LIMITED (SIADPL), we provide a comprehensive range of engineering, infrastructure and support services designed to meet the requirements of railway, government and private-sector projects. Our services are built around technical capability, quality workmanship, safety, timely execution and dependable project support.

We understand that every infrastructure project has different technical requirements, site conditions, schedules and operational challenges. Therefore, our approach is to provide practical and professionally managed solutions from project planning and site execution to maintenance and support.

Our major service areas include Railway Infrastructure, Signal & Telecommunication, Electrical Works, Civil Works, Maintenance & AMC, Manpower Supply and Material Supply.

Railway Infrastructure

Delivering reliable railway infrastructure solutions, including S&T, trackside systems, maintenance, and specialized works, ensuring safety, efficiency, connectivity, and seamless railway operations.

Electrical & Power Infrastructure

Providing dependable electrical and power infrastructure services, including installation, cabling, maintenance, and power systems, supporting safe, efficient, and uninterrupted operations.

Civil & General Infrastructure

Executing quality civil and general infrastructure projects, including construction, development, maintenance, and site works, with strong focus on durability, safety, and timely completion.

Manpower & Material Supply

Supplying skilled manpower and quality materials tailored to project requirements, ensuring reliable workforce support, timely availability, efficient execution, and uninterrupted project operations.
